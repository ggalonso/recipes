# **Chile Relleno**

* Prep Time: 30 minutes
* Cook Time: 5 minutes per batch
* Serving: 6
* Temperature: 365°F

## Overall Ingredients

<center>

| Ingredient         | Measurement        |
| ------------------ | ------------------ |
| Chili Peppers `*`  | 10                 |
| Neutral Oil        | 2 cups, for frying |
| Cheese `**`        | 1 cup              |
| Eggs               | 6                  |
| Flour              | 1/2 cup + 1 tbs    |
| Salt               | 3/4 tsp            |

</center>

`*` Poblano Chili Peppers are normally used. Personally I like using Hatch Green Peppers.<br>
`**` Personal choice of cheese. Personally I use Oaxaca Cheese, Mozzarella, or Muenster Cheese.

> **Note**<br>
> This recipe is for the Chile Rellenos only. No Sauce is added for the Chile Rellenos.

## Peppers Instructions

* While stems are intact to pepper, grill or broil pepper on each side until slightly blackened.
* Place peppers in large plastic bag and steam for 3 minutes.
* Skin each pepper and discard the skin.
* Cut a slit lengthwise along fat side of pepper.
* Cup each pepper and place in running water to remove seeds.
* Cut (long thin sticks) or shred cheese 
* Stuff chile with cheese into the pepper slit.

## Chile Relleno Batter Instructions

* Measure 1/2 cup flour into sifter and dust chiles well on all sides.
* Separate egg whites from egg yolks.
* Beat egg whites and a pinch of salt with electric mixture until peaks form.
* Pour 1 tbs of flour and 1 pinch of salt into egg yolk. Beat in another bowl.
* Pour yolk mixture into egg whites. Fold together lightly with rubber spatula.
* Use a rubber spatula and frost a stuffed floured chile.

## Cooking Instructions

* Pour 2 cups of oil in a large pan and heat until 365°F.
* While holding the chile by the stem, place carefully into oil.
* Repeat with additional Chiles.
* Fry 3 to 4 at a time.
* Fry the chiles until golden on both sides.
