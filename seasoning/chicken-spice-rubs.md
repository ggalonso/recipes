## **All Purpose Spice #1**

A simple, well-balanced seasoning with savory garlic and onion, complemented by paprika and a touch of black pepper. This versatile rub enhances the natural flavor of chicken without overpowering it, making it perfect for everyday cooking.

<center>

| Ingredient         | Measurement |
| ------------------ | ----------- |
| Garlic Powder      | 1/4 Cup     |
| Onion Powder       | 3 Tbsp      |
| Salt               | 2 Tbsp      |
| Black Pepper       | 2 Tbsp      |
| Paprika            | 3 Tbsp      |

</center>

## **All Purpose Spice #2**

A smoky, savory rub featuring smoked paprika and garlic, with earthy thyme and a mild kick of cayenne. It adds a rich, slightly peppery flavor to chicken and works well for grilling, roasting, or pan-searing.

<center>

| Ingredient         | Measurement |
| ------------------ | ----------- |
| Smoked Paprika     | 1/2 Cup     |
| Garlic Powder      | 1/4 Cup     |
| Salt               | 2 Tsp       |
| Black Pepper       | 1 Tsp       |
| Thyme              | 1 Tsp       |
| Ground Cayanne     | 1/4 Tsp     |

</center>

## **Smokey Chipotle**

A bold, smoky rub with deep smoked paprika, chipotle heat, and earthy cumin. Garlic, mustard, and black pepper add extra depth, creating a warm, slightly spicy flavor that is ideal for grilled or roasted chicken.

<center>

| Ingredient         | Measurement |
| ------------------ | ----------- |
| Smoked Paprika     | 1/2 Cup     |
| Garlic Powder      | 1/4 Cup     |
| Dry Mustard        | 1 Tsp       |
| Chili Powder       | 2 Tsp       |
| Cumin              | 1 Tsp       |
| Salt               | 2 Tsp       |
| Black Pepper       | 1 Tsp       |
| Smoked Chipotle    | 1 Tsp       |

</center>

## **Mediterranean Style**

A fragrant, herb-forward rub featuring rosemary, thyme, and garlic, brightened by fresh lemon zest. Black pepper and a touch of red pepper flakes provide a gentle kick, giving chicken a fresh, aromatic flavor inspired by Mediterranean cooking.

<center>

| Ingredient         | Measurement |
| ------------------ | ----------- |
| Dried Rosemary     | 1/2 Cup     |
| Thyme              | 1/3 Cup     |
| Black Pepper       | 1 Tsp       |
| Salt               | 2 Tsp       |
| Lemon Zest         | 1           |
| Garlic Powder      | 1/3 Cup     |
| Crushed Red Pepper | 1/3 Tsp     |

</center>

## **BBQ**

A sweet, smoky, and savory rub combining brown sugar, smoked paprika, garlic, and chili powder. Cumin, ginger, and dry mustard add warmth and depth, creating a classic barbecue-style flavor that is perfect for grilled, baked, or smoked chicken.

<center>

| Ingredient         | Measurement |
| ------------------ | ----------- |
| Smoked Paprika     | 1/4 Cup     |
| Garlic Powder      | 1/4 Cup     |
| Black Pepper       | 1/4 Cup     |
| Salt               | 1/4 Cup     |
| Brown Sugar        | 1/4 Cup     |
| Chili Powder       | 1/4 Cup     |
| Cumin              | 1/4 Cup     |
| Ginger             | 1/4 Cup     |
| Dried Mustard      | 1/4 Cup     |

</center>

## **Cajun Style**

A bold, spicy rub with smoked paprika, cayenne, black pepper, and garlic, balanced by earthy thyme and a savory saltiness. Crushed red pepper adds extra heat, making this blend ideal for chicken with a strong, zesty Cajun-inspired kick.

<center>

| Ingredient         | Measurement |
| ------------------ | ----------- |
| Smoked Paprika     | 1/4 Cup     |
| Cayanne            | 1/4 Cup     |
| Black Pepper       | 1/4 Cup     |
| Salt               | 1/4 Cup     |
| Thyme              | 1/4 Cup     |
| Garlic Powder      | 1/4 Cup     |
| Crushed Red Pepper | 1/4 Cup     |

</center>
